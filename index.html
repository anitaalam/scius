Scius is a boutique consulting firm based in Vancouver, Canada, focused on the most complex issues facing the real estate and construction sectors.

Our planet faces an unprecedented climate emergency, and we are running out of time. We work with leaders in business, government, and society to tackle these challenges — and to identify the many opportunities to future-proof their operations and thrive in a changing environment.
