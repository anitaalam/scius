Our story

How Scius
came to be.

Scius is a boutique consulting firm based in Vancouver, Canada, founded in 2018 to take on the most complex issues facing the real estate and construction sectors.

We began with a simple conviction: our planet faces an unprecedented climate emergency, and the built environment is both a major part of the problem and one of our biggest opportunities to fix it.

Since then we've worked with leaders across business, government, and society — mapping Canada's prefabrication sector, shaping policy and tools, and helping project teams move real innovation into the real world.
